- (decision) User wants a premium, modern, polished, highly usable Karachi-first real estate marketplace that reads as a professionally designed product, not a generic AI site or student project
- (decision) Avoid: excessive gradients, unnecessary glassmorphism, oversized rounded cards, random decorative elements, repetitive layouts, distracting animations
- (decision) Priorities: usability, hierarchy, clarity, consistency, accessibility; distinctive EstateAI identity; strong editorial layouts and high-quality property imagery where appropriate
- (decision) Must integrate conversational AI search, AI-assisted listing creation and price estimates naturally; interfaces suited to Individual, Agency and Administrator; responsive desktop and mobile
- (decision) Process: ask few preference questions, propose 2-3 distinct directions with mockups, user selects and refines one, then define design system, journeys, navigation and patterns; no frontend code; do not finalise a direction without user review
- (assumption) Sources: PRD + addendum, architecture spine, epics.md, decision register; frontend is Next.js 16 + Tailwind in separate repo; English-only UI, Karachi first, WCAG 2.1 AA for search, listing creation and chat
- (decision) Preferences (2026-11-19): personality = trusted and established; AI woven in quietly (helpers, not hero); references = classic property portals plus calm product software; identity = restrained (neutrals plus one confident accent, photography carries colour)
- (event) Three direction mockups rendered in .working/: direction-registry.html (paper and ink, serif, deep green #1F4D3A), direction-atlas.html (cool grey, blue-teal #0A6C92, list+map), direction-gallery.html (white and black, grotesk, terracotta #B4532F); shown to user, awaiting reaction. No direction chosen.
- (decision) User reaction 2026-11-19: all three directions acceptable, functionality first, UI refinement later. Provisional working direction for frontend stories = Atlas (calm product software, list plus map, ask-in-words bar; tokens: bg #EEF0F3, surface #FFFFFF, text #14181F, accent #0A6C92, system-ui sans with tabular numerals, radius max 6px). Registry typography and Gallery imagery treatment remain options for the later refinement pass. DESIGN.md and EXPERIENCE.md are not finalised.
