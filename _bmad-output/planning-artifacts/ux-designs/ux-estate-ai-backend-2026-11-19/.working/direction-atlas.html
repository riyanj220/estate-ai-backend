Rationale. Calm product software for a search-heavy task: cool greys, flat white surfaces, a single blue-teal accent and tabular numerals so prices and areas line up. Results sit beside a map in a split view, and AI is simply another input: an "Ask in words" bar that turns a sentence into chips you can edit, plus a range bar for estimates.

Trade-offs: The most efficient for comparing and refining, and the closest to a classic portal, but it is neutral in character and depends on the photography and map for warmth; the split layout is dense and collapses into a list/map toggle on mobile.
