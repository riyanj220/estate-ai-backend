EstateAI design direction A

Registry

Rationale. A trusted institution's register: warm paper, near-black ink and a single deep green, with serif headings and prices set against plain sans UI. Hairline rules replace cards and shadows so every listing reads like a precise ledger entry, and the AI shows up only as a quiet line of words and a ledger-style estimate.

Trade-offs: Highest information density and the most authoritative tone, but the least playful and least photo-forward; the serif and tight rows need care on small screens and may feel conservative to younger buyers.
