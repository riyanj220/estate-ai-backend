# Stories 10.1 to 10.3: account closure requests, execution and erasure (BE+FE)

Acceptance criteria: `epics.md`, Epic 10. Rules: FR-8, FR-74, AD-23. Owner direction: a final-year project; keep
governance simple; plain screens (the UI is redesigned later).

## API

| Route | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/v1/me/closure-requests` | `{can_submit, request}` (the latest) |
| `POST /api/v1/me/closure-requests` `{kind}` | `deactivation` or `deletion`; 201; 409 while one is pending; Suspended allowed |
| `GET /api/v1/me/closure-requests/{id}` | own only, else 404 |
| `GET /api/v1/admin/closure-requests?status&cursor` | queue, oldest first, masked email |
| `POST /api/v1/admin/closure-requests/{id}/approve` or `/reject` `{reason}` | 409 once decided |

Screens: `/account/closure` (linked from the profile page) and `/admin/closure-requests`.

## How an approval runs

One transaction in `governance.closure.approve_closure`: account lock, request lock, decision and audit,
`identity.services.deactivate`, then every `kernel.erasure` eraser from the top layer down. The email is queued last
and sent after commit. Any failure rolls all of it back.

| Eraser | Does |
|---|---|
| governance | anonymises Conversation Report snapshots (direct call); withdraws a pending and revokes an approved Verification; blanks the submitted details of every request; clears the verified flag |
| messaging | nothing: Messages are kept and the other side sees "Deleted user" |
| assistant | deletes Assistant sessions and turns |
| valuation | deletes the price estimates held under the account |
| listings | archives Published, Sold, Rented and Removed Listings through `status` and `moderation`; deletes private addresses and favourites; Drafts kept |
| identity | replaces email and profile fields, unusable password, deletes tokens and notifications |

## Decisions

- Both kinds are carried out the same way (PRD R11: deletion is deactivation with anonymisation). The kind is recorded.
- No free text from the requester, and no withdrawal by the requester (the administrator rejects instead).
- A reason (5 to 500 characters, no contact details) is required for both decisions; the account holder reads it, on
  the status page after a rejection and in the email for both. A rejection also raises an in-app notification.
- The email becomes `closed-<id>@closed.estate-ai.invalid`, so the old address can register again as a new account
  with no quota history. Phone numbers are not unique and carry nothing over.
- Verification Decision rows are append-only, so "evidence deleted" means the request rows stay with their submitted
  details blanked.
- Private addresses are deleted from all Listings, Drafts included (personal data). Titles, descriptions, images and
  public locations stay.
- A restriction stays on an archived Listing. `restore_after_moderation` now refuses a Deactivated owner (409);
  public visibility already required an Active owner.
- `identity.services.deactivate` now ends sessions inside its transaction (it also still emits the event).
- The closure request rows are kept; only the kind goes into audit metadata.

## Left for later Epic 10 stories

- 10.4 can show a closed account's requests (`governance.closure`); the queue shows only masked email and type.
- 10.7/10.12: the audit review and retention of closure requests (no retention job is defined for them).
- Free text of Listing Reports written by a closed account is not scrubbed (only Message snapshots are required).
