# Stories 10.11 to 10.13: retention and the audit purge (BE)

Acceptance criteria: `epics.md`, Epic 10. Rules: PRD 8.3, FR-69, AD-6, AD-23. No migrations, no API change.

## Built

- 10.11: the kernel jobs already existed (idempotency, rate limits, AI request log, notifications). Application logs
  are not stored by the app; the 30-day rule is documented as a deployment setting.
- 10.12: new jobs `listings.publication_events`, `assistant.published_history`, `governance.reports`,
  `governance.verification`, `governance.moderation_records`, `governance.support_requests`,
  `governance.closure_requests`.
- 10.13: database role `purger` (SELECT and DELETE on `PURGE_ROLE_TABLES` only), connection alias `purge` (only when
  `PURGE_PASSWORD` is set), command `purge_audit_records [--dry-run]`. The runtime role was already refused UPDATE and
  DELETE on the audit table.

Table of periods: `docs/runtime-and-retention.md`. Role setup, including an existing database: `docs/audit.md`.

## Decisions

- Moderation records are purged by `governance`, which owns them (the story text lists them under `listings`; AD-3).
- One purge role for all append-only history (audit, publication events, moderation, Report and Verification
  history), instead of giving the runtime role DELETE.
- Dry runs and runs with nothing to delete need no purge role.
- A Verification approval still in force keeps its row (status and revocation depend on it); only the registration
  number and note are emptied after 24 months. Other decided requests are deleted with their history.
- Closure requests: no period in the PRD; decided ones go 24 months after the decision, pending ones stay.
- Assistant history counts 90 days from the first Publication.
- The audit purge is its own operator command, not a `run_retention` job. It writes no audit record of itself.
- The 24-month age rule is enforced by the code; the database restricts which role and which tables, not row age.
- 24 and 12 months are calendar months.

## Left over

- Add the role to the existing development database (commands in `docs/audit.md`).
- Scheduling the two commands and log capture retention are decided at deployment.
- A job that deletes with the purge role commits on its own connection, and purges are unbatched (as before).
