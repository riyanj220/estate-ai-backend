# Epic 2 retrospective (light)

**Outcome:** all 21 stories merged in both repos and in `review`: Drafts with registry-driven fields, images, location with private address, amenities, preview and delete, quota policy and allowance, publish, sample data, lifecycle, republication, correction window, admin quota and suspension.

**Worked:** one publish path with a guard; privacy kept behind the projection and its tests; reviewers caught real defects (stuck published edits, drifted fingerprints, stale-save data loss, unretried notices).

**Cost:** full local test runs and three reviewers on every story were too slow. Changed: narrow local runs, full suites in CI (parallel), reviews scaled by risk.

**Lessons:** the changed-spec run misses cross-spec breakage, so check CI after every merge; the stub must mirror the backend's refusal order; sync the frontend contract whenever backend main moves, or CI fails.

**Open (carried):** polish items noted in the story triage logs (focus after publish, stub month rule, public list index in 3.4); retro items 3, 5, 6, 7 from Epic 1.

**Verdict:** accepted with open items.
